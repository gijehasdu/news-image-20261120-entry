# The World in One Day — 20 November 2026

Entry for make.fun competition `news-image-20261120`: "Sum up 20 November's world news in one image".

The image is a newspaper front page dated 20 November 2026, summing up that day's world news in six stories:

- Missiles over Poland — Russian missiles and a drone violate Polish airspace; NATO calls it a grave violation (20 November 2026).
- Kyiv bridges hit — Russian strikes hit three bridges in Kyiv after Ukraine struck the Crimean Bridge (20 November 2026).
- Myanmar genocide ruling — the World Court finds Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya (20 November 2026).
- Markets slump — the S&P 500 falls 3.7% into correction as AI shares sell off; the Nikkei drops 5% (20 November 2026).
- UK–EU sign new deals — food trade, carbon markets and youth mobility agreed at the Brussels summit (20 November 2026).
- BepiColombo reaches Mercury — the spacecraft arrives at Mercury after an eight-year journey (20 November 2026).

`entry.png` (1024×1024, JPEG bytes under the `entry.png` name) was produced from `front-page-source.png` (1600×1600), which is kept in this repository as its documented source, by resizing to 1024×1024 and re-encoding as JPEG at quality 85.

## How to run

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) checks that the bundled image is a PNG or JPEG within the 1 MiB limit, then copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, or to `/output/entry.png` when `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` is unset.
